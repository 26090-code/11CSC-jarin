FEATURED ISSUE

Biodiversity Loss

New Zealand’s unique ecosystem evolved in isolation, leading to remarkably high endemicity. However, this isolation leaves its native species highly vulnerable to human-driven habitat loss, agricultural intensification, and invasive predators. Now over three-quarters of native reptiles, birds, bats, and freshwater fish are threatened or at risk of extinction
